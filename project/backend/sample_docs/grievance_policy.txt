# Grievance Redressal Policy

Section 1: Filing a Grievance
Students may file a grievance through the online grievance portal or by submitting a written complaint to the grievance redressal officer. The grievance officer for the academic year 2024-25 is Professor R. Sharma, located in Room 104 of the Administrative Block.

Section 2: Response Time
Grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days and resolved within 15 working days. If a grievance is not resolved within this period, the student may escalate it to the Principal.

Section 3: Grievance Categories
Grievances may relate to academics, examinations, hostel, library, fees, or harassment. Each category is handled by a designated sub-committee that reports to the grievance redressal officer.

Section 4: Confidentiality
All grievances are treated with strict confidentiality. The identity of the complainant is protected and retaliation against complainants is treated as a serious disciplinary offence.
