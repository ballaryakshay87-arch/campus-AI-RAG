# Attendance Policy

Section 1: Minimum Attendance
Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated from the total number of classes conducted.

Section 2: Condonation
Students with attendance between 60% and 74% may apply for condonation on medical grounds with a valid medical certificate. A condonation fee of Rs. 1,000 per subject is charged. Students below 60% attendance are not eligible for condonation.

Section 3: Medical Leave
Medical leave is granted for a maximum of 10 days per semester on submission of a certificate from a registered medical practitioner. Medical leave does not count against attendance.

Section 4: Attendance Shortage
Students falling short of 75% attendance without valid condonation will not be permitted to write the end-semester examination and must repeat the course in the next available semester.
