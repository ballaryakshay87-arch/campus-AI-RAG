# Class Timetable 2024-25

Section 1: Class Hours
Classes are conducted in six periods per day. Period 1 is from 9:00 AM to 9:50 AM, Period 2 from 9:50 AM to 10:40 AM, Period 3 from 11:00 AM to 11:50 AM, Period 4 from 11:50 AM to 12:40 PM, Period 5 from 2:00 PM to 2:50 PM, and Period 6 from 2:50 PM to 3:40 PM.

Section 2: Breaks
There is a 20-minute tea break from 10:40 AM to 11:00 AM and a lunch break from 12:40 PM to 2:00 PM. No classes are scheduled during breaks.

Section 3: Friday Schedule
Friday afternoons from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM are reserved for club activities, seminars, and guest lectures. Attendance at these sessions is mandatory and counts toward the 75% attendance requirement.
