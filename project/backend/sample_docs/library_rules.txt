# Library Rules and Services

Section 1: Membership and Access
All enrolled students are automatically members of the college library. The library is open from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on all working days. Students must swipe their college ID card at the entrance.

Section 2: Borrowing Limits
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books at a time for a period of 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6 books for 21 days. Reference books and journals are not issued and must be read within the library.

Section 3: Overdue Fines
A fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged for each overdue book. If a book is overdue by more than 30 days, borrowing privileges are suspended until the book is returned and the fine is paid.

Section 4: Lost Books
If a borrowed book is lost, the student must either replace it with the same or latest edition or pay twice the current market price of the book.
