# Internship Policy

Section 1: Duration and Timing
Every undergraduate student must complete a mandatory internship of at least 6 weeks duration. The internship is typically undertaken at the end of the sixth semester during the summer break.

Section 2: Approval Process
Students must get their internship offer and project plan approved by the faculty internship coordinator at least 30 days before the start date. Internships at unapproved organisations are not credited.

Section 3: Evaluation
Internships are evaluated based on a supervisor's report, a student submission report, and a presentation. The internship carries 2 credits and the grade appears on the transcript as Internship (IN).

Section 4: Stipend
The college does not guarantee a stipend for internships. However, students receiving a stipend of more than Rs. 10,000 per month must inform the placement cell for record-keeping.
